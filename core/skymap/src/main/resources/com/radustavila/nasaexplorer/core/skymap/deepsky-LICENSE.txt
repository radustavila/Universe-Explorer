Changes: only objects of visual magnitude below 12 are kept (single and double stars and duplicate
entries left out), plus every Messier and Caldwell object. Each keeps its name, first common name,
type (grouped into eight kinds), right ascension and declination (J2000), constellation, visual
magnitude (blue where no visual one is given), major and minor axes, position angle and Messier
number. Caldwell numbers were added by hand from NASA's Hubble Caldwell Catalog, M102 is taken as
NGC 5866, five bright Caldwell clusters without a magnitude and the Coalsack without a size get
the Caldwell catalogue's values, and the result is written in the binary format of core/skymap/tools/build_sky_catalog.py.
